What is Dance Movement Therapy?

Where Movement Meets Healing

Dance Movement Therapy (DMT) uses movement within a creative process which addresses the psychophysical wellbeing of the person and does so by establishing a therapeutic relationship which goes beyond the meeting of two minds.

DMT is an interdisciplinary profession based on the union of motion and emotion. It draws from non-verbal communication, developmental psychology, neurosciences, group dynamics, and theories from different psychological orientations.
