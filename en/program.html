Master's Programme

A two-year, 120 ECTS programme combining rigorous theory, experiential learning, clinical practice, and research.

Programme Overview

Comprehensive DMT Training

The Master's Degree in Dance Movement Therapy at UAB is the only complete university training programme in DMT recognised by the European Association of Dance Movement Therapy (EADMT) and the Spanish ADMTE.

The programme follows the directives of the Spanish Dance Movement Therapy Association (ADMTE) and consolidates programmes from Britain and the USA, adapted to the European context and specifically to the Spanish and Catalan cultural environment.

Students receive a thorough grounding in psychotherapeutic theory while developing their own movement practice and clinical skills through supervised placements.

Curriculum Structure

The programme is organised into four interconnected areas that develop throughout both years.

01

Theoretical Foundations

Psychotherapeutic theory from different psychological orientations, developmental psychology, neurosciences, movement analysis systems (Laban Movement Analysis, Kestenberg Movement Profile), and research methodology. Students gain a deep understanding of the theoretical underpinnings of DMT practice.

Core Module · Year 1 & 2
02

Experiential Learning & Personal Process

Personal process through movement, embodiment practices, and DMT preparation experiential groups. Students engage in their own therapeutic process through movement, developing self-awareness and embodied understanding essential for clinical work.

Practical Module · Year 1 & 2
03

Clinical Practice & Supervision

Over 300 hours of annual clinical practice with diverse populations under professional supervision. Students work in hospitals, mental health centres, schools, geriatric facilities, and community settings, applying their learning in real therapeutic contexts.

300+ Hours/Year · Year 1 & 2
04

Research Project

Students develop and carry out a compulsory research project integrating theory and clinical experience. The project contributes to the growing body of evidence-based research in Dance Movement Therapy and demonstrates the student's capacity for independent academic work.

Research Module · Year 2
05

Professional Development

Ethics, professional standards, and the development of a professional identity as a dance movement therapist. Students learn about the regulatory framework, professional associations, and the requirements for independent practice after graduation.

Professional Module · Year 2

Year by Year

1

First Year

The first year focuses on building foundational knowledge and beginning clinical work:

  • Theory Foundations & Methods
  • Movement Analysis LMA & KMP
  • Personal Process Group & Individual
  • Clinical Practice 300+ Hours
  • Supervision Weekly Sessions
2

Second Year

The second year deepens clinical expertise and focuses on research:

  • Advanced Theory Specialisation Topics
  • Clinical Practice 300+ Hours
  • Research Master's Project
  • Professional Dev. Ethics & Standards
  • Supervision Weekly Sessions

Learning Outcomes

Upon completion, graduates will be able to:

✓

Assess & Plan

Conduct DMT assessments using movement observation and analysis tools, and design appropriate therapeutic interventions for diverse populations and settings.

✓

Facilitate & Reflect

Facilitate individual and group DMT sessions with clinical awareness, maintaining a reflective practice through supervision and personal process work.

✓

Research & Contribute

Design and carry out research in DMT, contributing to evidence-based practice and the professional development of the field.
