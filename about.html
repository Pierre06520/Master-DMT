What is Dance Movement Therapy?

An interdisciplinary profession at the intersection of movement, creativity, and psychotherapy.

Understanding DMT

The Union of Motion & Emotion

Dance Movement Therapy (DMT) uses movement within a creative process which addresses the psychophysical wellbeing of the person and does so by establishing a therapeutic relationship which goes beyond the meeting of two minds.

DMT is an interdisciplinary profession based on the union of motion and emotion: it uses psychotherapeutic theory and methods and is based on research into non-verbal communication, developmental psychology, and systems of analysis for movement.

As a form of creative arts therapy, DMT has its roots in the observation that movement reflects inner emotional states and that changes in movement behaviour can lead to changes in the psyche, promoting health and growth.

♫

Foundations of DMT

Dance Movement Therapy draws from multiple disciplines to create a holistic approach to healing.

🧠

Developmental Psychology

Understanding human development through the lens of movement patterns that emerge from infancy through adulthood. Early movement patterns reflect psychological development and can be accessed therapeutically.

💬

Non-verbal Communication

The body communicates what words cannot. DMT works with the rich vocabulary of non-verbal expression—gesture, posture, spatial relationships, and movement qualities—to access deeper layers of experience.

🧩

Neurosciences

Current neuroscientific research supports the mind-body connection at the heart of DMT. Embodied cognition, mirror neurons, and somatic markers demonstrate how movement influences mental and emotional states.

👥

Group Dynamics

DMT often works within group settings, drawing on theories of group process and interpersonal relationships. The group becomes a microcosm of society where relational patterns can be explored and transformed through movement.

🎭

Creative Process

Creativity is at the heart of DMT. Through improvised and structured movement, clients access their innate creative resources, finding new ways to express, explore, and resolve emotional challenges.

💖

Therapeutic Relationship

The therapeutic relationship in DMT goes beyond verbal exchange. Through shared movement, mirroring, and empathic engagement, therapist and client build a relationship that supports deep therapeutic work.

Who Benefits from DMT?

Dance Movement Therapy serves diverse populations across clinical, educational, and community settings.

👶

Children & Adolescents

DMT supports children with developmental challenges, autism spectrum conditions, behavioural difficulties, and emotional regulation. Movement provides a natural medium for children to express and process experiences.

🧓

Adults

Adults benefit from DMT for anxiety, depression, trauma, eating disorders, chronic pain, and personal development. The embodied approach reaches aspects of experience that talk therapy alone may not access.

👴

Older Adults

DMT offers older adults opportunities for social connection, cognitive stimulation, and emotional expression. It has shown particular promise with dementia, Parkinson's disease, and age-related isolation.

🏠

Community & Social Settings

Beyond clinical contexts, DMT is used in schools, refugee centres, prisons, and community organisations. It promotes social inclusion, cultural integration, and collective wellbeing through the universal language of movement.

History of DMT at UAB

Over two decades of excellence in Dance Movement Therapy education.

2003

Programme Founded

The Master's programme began following the directives of the Spanish Dance Movement Therapy Association (ADMTE) and consolidated programmes from Britain and the USA.

2007

ECARTE Membership

The programme became part of the European Consortium for Arts in Education (ECARTE), the European network for university studies in creative therapies.

2013

10th Anniversary

Celebration of 10 years of DMT training at UAB with an international conference and publication of commemorative materials.

2018

15th Anniversary

Marking 15 years of continuous training, with students from over 30 countries having graduated from the programme.

2022

Programme Extension to 120 ECTS

On recommendation of the European Association of DMT (EADMT), the programme was extended to 120 ECTS credits with enhanced research and clinical components.

Today

22+ Editions & Growing

The programme continues to evolve, maintaining its position as the reference university DMT training in Spain and southern Europe.
