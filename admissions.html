Admissions

Join the next edition of Europe's leading university programme in Dance Movement Therapy.

Entry Requirements

The programme welcomes applicants from diverse academic and professional backgrounds.

🎓

Academic Requirements

Applicants should hold a bachelor's degree (or equivalent) in one of the following areas or related disciplines:

  • Psychology ✓
  • Education / Pedagogy ✓
  • Medicine / Health Sciences ✓
  • Social Work ✓
  • Dance / Performing Arts ✓
  • Fine Arts ✓
💃

Personal Requirements

Beyond academic qualifications, the selection process considers:

  • Movement Experience Required
  • Personal Therapy Recommended
  • Spanish Language B2 Level
  • Motivation & Commitment Essential
  • Interview Required

Application Process

Follow these steps to apply for the Master's programme.

01

Pre-registration

Complete the online pre-registration form through the UAB postgraduate portal. A pre-registration fee of €30.21 applies. This reserves your place in the selection process.

Online · UAB Portal
02

Documentation

Submit your academic transcripts, CV, motivation letter, and evidence of movement experience. International students may need to provide official translations and credential equivalency documents.

Documents Required
03

Interview & Movement Session

Shortlisted candidates are invited to a personal interview and a group movement session. This is an essential part of the selection process, allowing the team to assess your suitability for the programme and for working with movement therapeutically.

In-person or Online
04

Admission Decision

Candidates are notified of the admission decision. Successful applicants confirm their place by completing the enrolment process and paying the first instalment of the programme fee.

Notification by Email

Frequently Asked Questions

While formal dance training is not strictly required, you do need significant experience with movement. This can include dance, martial arts, yoga, somatic practices, or other embodied disciplines. The key is that you have a developed relationship with movement as a form of expression and self-knowledge.
The primary language of instruction is Spanish. A B2 level of Spanish is required for admission. Some reading materials and guest lectures may be in English. The international character of the student group means that multiple languages are present informally.
The programme is demanding, with classes, clinical placements, personal process work, and research commitments. While some students do maintain part-time work, the programme requires significant time and emotional investment. We recommend discussing your situation with the programme coordinators before enrolling.
Graduates can register as professional dance movement therapists with the ADMTE (Spain) and EADMT (Europe). Career opportunities include working in mental health settings, hospitals, schools, geriatric centres, private practice, community organisations, and academic research. Many graduates combine clinical work with teaching and research.
The programme has established partnerships with a range of clinical settings in the Barcelona area, including hospitals, mental health centres, schools, and community organisations. Students are placed according to their interests and learning needs, with ongoing supervision provided throughout.
