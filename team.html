Teaching Team

An international team of experienced professionals, researchers, and clinicians in Dance Movement Therapy.

Programme Direction

👤

Dr. Heidrun Panhofer

Programme Director

Senior Lecturer at UAB, registered Dance Movement Therapist (ADTR, RDMT), and researcher in DMT. Has led the programme since its founding in 2003 and is recognised internationally for her contributions to the field.

👤

Dr. Iris Brawer

Academic Coordinator

Dance Movement Therapist and researcher specialising in DMT with children and adolescents. Coordinates the academic curriculum and oversees the integration of theory with clinical practice.

👤

Clinical Supervision Team

Supervision Coordinators

A team of experienced registered dance movement therapists who coordinate and provide clinical supervision across all placement settings, ensuring quality and professional development.

Teaching Faculty

Our faculty brings together expertise from dance movement therapy, psychology, neuroscience, and the creative arts.

🎓

DMT Specialists

Registered dance movement therapists with extensive clinical experience across diverse populations, including mental health, special education, geriatrics, and community settings.

🧠

Psychologists & Researchers

Clinical psychologists and academic researchers who contribute expertise in developmental psychology, neuroscience, psychotherapy theory, and research methodology.

🎭

Movement Analysts

Certified Movement Analysts (CMA) who teach Laban Movement Analysis and Kestenberg Movement Profile, essential tools for movement observation and assessment in DMT.

International Guest Lecturers

The programme regularly invites leading figures from the international DMT community.

Global Perspectives

Learning from World Leaders

As part of the ECARTE network, the programme benefits from connections with universities and DMT programmes across Europe and beyond. International guest lecturers bring diverse perspectives and specialisations.

Guest lecturers have included leading DMT professionals, researchers, and educators from the UK, USA, Germany, Israel, and other countries, enriching students' learning with international best practice.
